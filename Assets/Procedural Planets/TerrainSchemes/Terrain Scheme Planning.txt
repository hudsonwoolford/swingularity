Continental Earth
- Existing setup
- Earth-like terrain planets
    - Grass, desert, forests and snow
- Mountains and valleys, and equal oceam

Cartoon Moon
- Smooth, spherical moon with craters
- Overemphasised craters
- Not much other noise
- Simple colours
    - Smooth non-metallic pastel colours like pink, blue and yellow and grey

Odd Asteroid
- Lots of shape noise
- Lots of deforming craters
- Odd, pockmarked shapes
- Some metallic colours and rocky colours
    - Give the idea of ore etc.

Standard Moon
- Little - no shape noise
- Some craters
- Some ridges and Mountains
- Rocky colours and shades of grey
    - Potentially some metallic

Reverse ridge
- Negative ridge noise elevation
- Mountains and valleys
- Icy/lava colours

Ocean Islands
- Majority of of space taken over by oceans, only small islands remain
- Crater islands, mountain ranges, warped island strips
- Earth like colours, non-metallic, polar

Two Faced
- Biome parameters create a divided planet, each biome on a different side
- Terrain can be anything, but would look best with a planet that has lots of land
- Contrasting biomes, fire-ice, green-purple, magma-dessolate, desert-jungle, no polar

Cyclopian
- Enormous singular crater that gouges the planet
- Maxed crater settings and warped ridges surround it
- Lends itself to many styles, cratered jungle, fire planet. Metallic could work here. No polar.

Volcano
- Towering mountains and a reverse - ridged terrain
- Narrow and wide valleys create channels for lava to flow through
- Volcano could be any colour, green, purple, red. High elevation would match ocean colours

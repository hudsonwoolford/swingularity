1. Continental Earth - DONE
- Existing setup
- Earth-like terrain planets
    - Grass, desert, forests and snow
- Mountains and valleys, and equal oceam

2. Cartoon Moon - DONE
- Smooth, spherical moon with craters
- Overemphasised craters
- Not much other noise
- Simple colours
    - Smooth non-metallic pastel colours like pink, blue and yellow and grey

3. Lumpy Asteroid - DONE
- Lots of shape noise
- Lots of deforming craters
- Odd, pockmarked shapes
- Some metallic colours and rocky colours
    - Give the idea of ore etc.

4. Standard Moon - DONE
- Little - no shape noise
- Some craters
- Some ridges and Mountains
- Rocky colours and shades of grey
    - Potentially some metallic

5. Reverse ridge/ submerged tectonics
- Negative ridge noise elevation
- Mountains and valleys
- Icy/lava colours

6. Ridge mars - DONE
- Yellows and oranges 
- Ridge noise mountains
- Little craters
- Can have ice poles

7. Ocean Archapelago - DONE
- Majority of of space taken over by oceans, only small islands remain
- Crater islands, mountain ranges, warped island strips
- Earth like colours, non-metallic, polar

8. Two Faced - DONE
- Biome parameters create a divided planet, each biome on a different side
- Terrain can be anything, but would look best with a planet that has lots of land
- Contrasting biomes, fire-ice, green-purple, magma-dessolate, desert-jungle, no polar

9. Cyclopian - DONE
- Enormous singular crater that gouges the planet
- Maxed crater settings and warped ridges surround it
- Lends itself to many styles, cratered jungle, fire planet. Metallic could work here. No polar.

10. Volcano/Warped rivers - DONE
- Towering mountains and a reverse - ridged terrain
- Narrow and wide valleys create channels for lava to flow through
- Volcano could be any colour, green, purple, red. High elevation would match ocean colours

11. Spiral warp - DONE
- Large amounts of warp noise
- Some mountains and valleys
- Lends itself to magma/purple-green colours

12. Terrestrial craters - DONE
- Cratered/apocalyptic Earth
- Terrestrial colours + magma or toxin

13. Oasis Dunes - DONE
- Dune like deserts
- Small pockets of water
- Obvs desert colours but could also do lava etc.

14. Rocky Esker Terrestrial
- Standard earth with mountains and valleys but also including ridges and rock formations
- Rocks as trees
- Grey + mossy + Terrestrial colour schemes

15. Crystal Smooth
- Crystal formations/normal maps/valleys and mountains
- Crystalline colors such as shimmering blues, greens, and purples
    - High smoothness and light metallic
- Not much noise, rely on normal mapping

16. Abyssal Terrain
- Really deep craters and black/no ocean
- Seems like holes in the surface
- Blacks, greys, purples and reds

17. Flat Terrestrial
- Small/no mountains
- Small ridges perhaps
- Terrestrial colours/biomes

18. Blobby
- Blobby spherical outcroppings
- Garish colours
    - Greens, blues, pinks and purples

19. Soft Moon
- Very small craters
- Little to no mountains and valleys
- Rocky colours

20. Canyon moon - DONE (REQUIRES EXTRA CODING FOR SEABED WITHOUT OCEAN THOUGH)
- Large, wide depressions with no ocean
- Rocky or sandy colours
- No ocean but still coloured sea bed perhaps
